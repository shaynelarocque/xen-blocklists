Xen Blocklists

Content-blocker lists for Xen Browser, rebuilt every week from their authors' filter lists and fetched by the app. manifest.json names the current lists.

The lists are the work of their authors, under their licences:

  • EasyList, EasyPrivacy, EasyList Cookie List, Fanboy's lists: GPLv3 or CC BY-SA 3.0
  • uBlock Origin filters: GPLv3
  • AdGuard filters: GPLv3
  • Peter Lowe's list: terms on its site
